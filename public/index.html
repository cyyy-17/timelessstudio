06 / DETAILS

Frequently Asked Questions

Clear, transparent answers about our freelance process, booking timeline, and deliverables.

Where are you based and how does on-location freelance coverage work? +

We are based in Barangay San Juan, Malvar, Batangas. As a freelance production team, we travel directly to your preparation hotel, church, reception venue, home, or outdoor photoshoot location with full cinema cameras, lighting, and audio equipment.

How far in advance should we inquire for a wedding or 18th debut? +

We recommend inquiring 3 to 6 months in advance for peak celebration season (October through February, and May). Because we never double-book dates, our crew calendar fills quickly. Inquiries for short-notice dates are also welcomed whenever calendar space permits.

Do you cover events outside of Malvar and Batangas Province? +

Yes. In addition to Malvar, Lipa, Tanauan, and Batangas City, we frequently shoot throughout Tagaytay, Cavite, Laguna, Quezon Province, Metro Manila, and destination weddings across the Philippines. Out-of-town travel arrangements are outlined transparently in your quote.

What is your reservation and payment policy? +

A standard reservation deposit officially locks your date on our schedule. The remaining balance is split across convenient milestones (e.g., during the prenup/pre-debut session and on event day). We accept GCash, direct bank transfer (BDO/BPI), and cash.

What is the turnaround time for our Same-Day Edit (SDE) and final photos? +

The Same-Day Edit (SDE) video is edited on-site during your wedding or debut and projected live at your evening reception. Complete color-graded high-resolution photos and the cinematic highlights reel are delivered via a private cloud link within 3 to 4 weeks. Physical storybook albums are printed and delivered within 6 to 8 weeks.
